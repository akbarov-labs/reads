# Site-wide rebrand: Reads becomes Kitoblarim — katalog

Scope: every public page (header, footer, home, listings, detail pages, OG and icons) and the reads-admin panel name. Visitor mode: Read (a reader judging a book). Layout and content stay; identity changes.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One product, one name. The catalogue looks like it was printed by the same hand as the Kitoblarim bot and landing, so following a book from here into Telegram feels like turning a page rather than leaving the site. Refuses the generic amber-on-stone "literary site" look and a second brand.

OWN-WORLD: Kitoblarim's pinned world. Bone Paper ground (#F6F7F4), green-tinted ink neutrals (#141A16 to #626B65), Deep Forest Green (#1F4D2E) as the only accent, with sage (#4A7D57) and a gold star (#D0951A) for ratings only. Onest for UI and Literata for titles, both self-hosted. The book-spine K mark in its green tile.

STORY: Readers see Kitoblarim at once, find and judge a book, and the hand-off ("Boshlagan kitobingizni Telegramda oxirigacha o'qing") reads as the same brand's next step.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The header lockup (mark tile + "Kitoblarim" + muted "katalog"), then the hero headline in Literata on a paper ground, with no kicker and no decorative gradient. The stats row stays, as one plain surface. The primary path is browsing; the bot link sits on book pages and in the footer.

FORM: pinned by owner (rename + re-skin, keep layout); no concept roll. Seed: none (brief-pinned).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
